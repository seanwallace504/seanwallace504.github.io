I'm a graduate student in Instructional Technology and Design at UNC Greensboro, interested in how technology, research, and creative practice can support meaningful learning.

My interests include instructional design, learning sciences, creative technology, music, and educational equity. I'm especially interested in making learning and technology more accessible, collaborative, and responsive to the people using them.

## Areas of Interest

### Instructional Design & Learning Sciences

I'm interested in how people learn and how research can inform the design of meaningful educational experiences. My work explores learning theories, instructional strategies, and ways to connect research findings with practical teaching and learning.

*More to come: learning theories, instructional design projects, and approaches to learning.*

### Research & Educational Equity

I value research that helps us better understand learners, educators, and the environments in which learning takes place. I'm particularly interested in how educational practices can support diverse learners and make quality learning opportunities more accessible.

*More to come: research experience, collaborative projects, and questions I'm exploring.*

### Creative Technology & Music

My background in jazz studies continues to shape how I think about creativity, collaboration, and learning. I'm interested in bringing together music, technology, visual thinking, and hands-on exploration to create opportunities for people to experiment and develop their own ideas.

*More to come: creative projects, music education, and technology experiments.*

## How I Approach Learning and Design

I believe effective learning experiences should give people opportunities to explore, ask questions, make connections, and build understanding. Technology is most valuable when it serves a meaningful purpose rather than becoming an end in itself.

I'm interested in approaches that encourage curiosity, collaboration, and experimentation while recognizing the different experiences and perspectives learners bring with them.

## Current Projects & Future Directions

- **Learning sciences:** Exploring how learning theory can inform instructional design decisions.
- **Educational research:** Investigating student experiences and the role of embodied learning.
- **Creative technology:** Developing projects that combine accessible tools, hands-on experimentation, and creative expression.
- **Professional development:** Connecting research, instructional design, and educational equity in future academic and community-based work.

## Beyond the Classroom

Outside of my graduate work, I'm interested in music, independent game development, open-source technology, and projects that encourage people to create and learn together.

I'm always looking for connections between seemingly different fields and new ways to turn ideas into something tangible.
