I’m originally from New Orleans, where I grew up and studied Jazz Studies at the University of New Orleans. After graduation, I found myself in Asheville, North Carolina. After spending some time working in the area, I decided I wanted to return to school. I’m currently a graduate student in Instructional Technology and Design at UNC Greensboro, interested in how technology, research, and creative practice can support meaningful learning.

My interests include instructional design, learning sciences, creative technology, music, and educational equity. I’m especially interested in making learning and technology more accessible, collaborative, and responsive to the people using them.

Community, culture, and a sense of belonging are things I took for granted growing up in New Orleans. They helped shape who I am, providing perspectives and opportunities I might not otherwise have encountered, and I remain grateful for them. My overall approach is to design environments in which community, purpose, and a sense of self can thrive naturally. I believe these things are already present in people and communities; by building environments grounded in respect, memory, and trust, we can foster new perspectives and values that bring us closer together.

*This page is a work in progress and will continue to grow alongside my academic, professional, and creative work.*
