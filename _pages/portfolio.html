Fragments II

Fragments II is a story-driven role-playing game set in a world being slowly consumed by an ancient and intelligent darkness known as the Veil. At its core, the game explores unity, corruption, and the cost of power, asking whether darkness is something that happens to people or something they invite in, one small compromise at a time.

The game draws inspiration from the emotional weight of Kingdom Hearts, the corruption arc of The Lord of the Rings, and the consuming existential threat of The Neverending Story. It aims to tell a story that is mythic in scale but personal in execution: a world-ending threat experienced through individuals in small towns, quiet moments, and conversations with people who don't fully understand what is happening to them.

What you're playing here is the prologue.

Due to time constraints, character and world visuals use default RPG Maker assets, with the exception of the title screen. The narrative, dialogue, and game mechanics are original.

Controls

  • Move: Arrow keys or left-click
  • Action / Confirm: Z, Enter, or left-click
  • Back: X
  • Menu: Esc
